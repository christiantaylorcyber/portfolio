01 / About

I work on both sides of security: offense and defense.

I'm a cybersecurity specialist at Secure Halo with a B.S. in Cybersecurity from BYU–Idaho. My work spans governance, risk & compliance, third-party risk management, and building incident response programs that hold up when something actually happens.

I run penetration tests, social engineering engagements, and phishing campaigns for clients, along with dark web monitoring and vulnerability scanning. Before that, I triaged alerts as a Tier 1 SOC analyst using Splunk, Security Onion, and Wireshark. Seeing both sides means I write controls knowing how they'll be tested and where they'll break. I also enjoy explaining technical findings to people who aren't technical.
